## Overview

**MedDiary** is a smart health-management app concept that helps people stay on top of medication schedules, appointments and medical records — for themselves or for loved ones. Its goal: reduce hospital crowding, prevent missed treatments and give families peace of mind.

I built the marketing website that introduces it: hand-written **HTML**, **CSS** and **JavaScript**, fully responsive.

## Features showcased

- **Medicine Educator** — usage, side effects and best practices for prescriptions
- **Medication Reminder** — never miss a dose
- **Digital Health Record** — history and reports in one place
- **Treatment Tracking & Follow-ups**
