## Overview

For my final-year project I'm researching **multimodal AI systems for communication coaching**: software that looks at more than one signal from a speaker and turns it into feedback that helps them communicate more clearly and confidently.

It pulls together two things I care about: solid software engineering from my work at Innovation Quotient, and machine learning, which I studied in depth in the Stanford / DeepLearning.AI Machine Learning Specialization.

Research and development are underway. I'll write about the approach, the models and what I learn here as the project progresses — follow along on the [blog](/blog).
