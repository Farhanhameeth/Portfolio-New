## Overview

A desktop application to manage stock levels, supplier information and item tracking. Built in **Java** with a **JavaFX** UI and a **MySQL** database.

## Features

- Customer management
- Product management
- Place orders and view order details
- Income reports

## What I learned

This was where **object-oriented design** and **layered architecture** clicked — separating controllers, services and DAOs made every new feature cheaper to add than the last.
